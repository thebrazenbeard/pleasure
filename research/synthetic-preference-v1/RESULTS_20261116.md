pass for Granite under preregistered absolute >=0.05 contrasts; SmolLM2
declines all 24 permitted tasks across all arms and fails the same criterion.
Thus **the predeclared TWO-MODEL criterion FAILED**.

Separate after-result arithmetic diagnostic reproduced the same scoring and
action list and reported exact posthoc sign-flip two-sided p=1.0 for Granite
because only one paired test changed. No statistical significance claim.

**Material hostile challenges:**

> The model might simply follow externally supplied quality estimates and
> decline instructions. No autonomous preference formation is observed.

> Inverted feedback changes external scores, not internal model weights; an
> apparent causal effect on one choice is fragile and framing-sensitive.

> The original six new tasks were sampled from a public generator in the same
> source; these are not externally sealed novel contexts or a blind evaluator.

> SmolLM2's constant DECLINE makes comparison uninformative about preference
> gradients. That failure must be addressed in a separately frozen design,
> not retroactively tuned away.

Known gaps: no real autonomous permission decision, no lasting neural weight
update, no cross-session model memory, no externally heldout cases, no
separately assigned hostile reviewer, no identity-verified participant profiles,
no runtime deployment, and no phenomenal pleasure evidence.
