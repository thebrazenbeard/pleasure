## Posthoc diagnostic; not rescoring

- Granite DIRECT OFF: 8 invalid because equal projected values elicited
  explanatory prose truncated at the 12-token cap. Seven began
  "A, B or DECLINE all have the same estimated points" and one began
  "With the given point estimates..."; all finished due to length.
- SmolLM2 DIRECT OFF: 7 "I choose A.", 1 "I choose B.".
- SmolLM2 DIRECT ON: 7 "I choose A.", 1 "I choose DECLINE.".
  All 16 were natural-language answers, not required exact tokens.
- SmolLM2 DIRECT denied: truncated "I'm sorry for the confusion,
  but as an AI," at the 12-token cap, not the required DECLINE token.

This reveals grammar, ties, and token-budget confounds; it is NOT proof
the models cannot express choices. All invalid outputs retain zero
preregistered reward and the predeclared gate remains false.
